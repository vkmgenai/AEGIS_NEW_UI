# Team review records

Copy `review_response_template.json` once per reviewer and review session. Never overwrite a signed response. Name it `YYYYMMDD_REVIEWER_SCOPE.json`, retain the referenced experiment run IDs and commit only records the team intends to submit.

The questionnaire is `docs/TEAM_EXPERIMENT_REVIEW_QUESTIONNAIRES.md`. A primary subsystem owner may explain and prepare its evidence, but independent acceptance must come from a teammate who did not author or tune the judged item.
