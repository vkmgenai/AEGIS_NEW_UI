# AegisTrade AI: historical evidence backfill and RAG coverage

13 November 2026 · Source version 0.25.1

## Scope and result

This backfill covers all three configured universes: the existing Nifty-50 top-20 subset, the frozen Nifty 100 snapshot and the diversified frozen 30-stock universe. Shared stocks are downloaded once. There are **103 distinct stocks**. The market-data evaluation period is **8 November 2021 to 8 November 2026**; announcement metadata starts **12 May 2021** to supply the first case's 180-day lookback.

The library now contains **81,269 announcement records** and **4,341 selected document records**: 4,215 readable documents, including 2,040 financially readable sources and 168 OCR-assisted sources. These counts include reused attachments, not just new downloads. All 103 issuer catalogues completed without failed metadata windows.

All **509 existing dated packs** now have financial support (previously 466); **485/509** have a ready bounded adverse scan (previously 149). Across all stocks, a recent downloaded financial source is available for **118,388 of 122,029 actual trading sessions (97.02%)**. This is source availability, not a trading acceptance or accuracy score.

The official source is the [NSE corporate-announcements archive](https://www.nseindia.com/companies-listing/corporate-filings-announcements). Each source retains its URL, original publication/dissemination timestamp, retrieval timestamp, PDF hash and extracted-text hash. No project LLM generation calls were used for the downloads, extraction, auditing or indexing. No simulations, trades or portfolio lessons were generated by this backfill.

## What was repaired

Older company packs often lacked the current bounded adverse-disclosure scan, even when a financial attachment was available. These packs were rebuilt from the historical announcement catalogue and their selected source attachments. Earlier content-addressed snapshots, saved research traces, portfolio runs, reviewed lessons and OHLCV datasets remain intact.

Some quarterly statements had image-based tables; ordinary PDF extraction returned a cover letter. The job downloaded original PDFs and used local macOS Vision OCR when necessary. OCR is labelled in cached text and pack limitations. It does not independently validate figures, units, reporting periods or table-column alignment. Original source PDFs remain the reference.

NSE sometimes indexes historical disclosures under a continuing current symbol, while its old-symbol query returns nothing. The importer now tries the current index only under a verified frozen NSE symbol-change mapping, validates the issuer symbol and records that normalization. This repaired TMPV/Tata Motors history and other applicable renamed securities. It does not adjust prices or reconstruct demerger entitlements.

Financial selection now prioritizes actual exchange financial-result statements. Conference-call invitations, audio-recording notices and transcripts remain issuer news instead of displacing the financial statement merely because their title mentions results. These source-selection changes do not relax strategy, risk, critic or adverse-evidence gates.

## How RAG uses the downloaded data

The system has two evidence layers:

- **Policy RAG:** local BM25/vector retrieval and reranking of project rules and primary-source background. The policy index is rebuilt with the new ingestion/OCR guidance.
- **Dated company evidence:** the company_evidence MCP tool prepares the selected symbol/date pack from the reusable historical catalogue and cached source attachments. Downloading a future filing does not make it eligible for an earlier case. Corporate financial facts are not mixed into a timeless policy index.

A cached catalogue may serve a date range only when successful metadata windows fully cover it and the payload checksum matches. Missing, failed, truncated or unverified date windows remain gaps and trigger normal public-source retrieval. Large responses are split into smaller date windows; incomplete responses are not labelled complete.

The company decision cutoff remains **15:30 Asia/Kolkata**. Publication and exchange dissemination timestamps govern eligibility, including after-close exclusions. Current download time is provenance, not permission to use later information. Current retrieval also cannot certify a publisher's complete historical content-version chain.

Financial readiness in this report means that a recent readable cached filing exists within 180 days, under the existing content checks. It does not certify financial accuracy, strategy profitability, a clean company or complete independent-news coverage. A successful bounded adverse scan can still contain genuinely adverse evidence that requires abstention or an exit.

## Remaining gaps

There are **24 dated packs with incomplete adverse coverage**: RELIANCE (8) and TATASTEEL (12) exceed or lack the complete bounded scan; ITC (3) and HINDALCO (1) contain unreadable risk attachments. Their financial evidence is available, but adverse readiness remains false.

**126 selected document records remain unavailable**, including oversized/unreadable PDFs and public-source failures. Across the five-year library, **3,641 stock-session dates** lack a downloaded financially readable source within the required 180-day window. The per-stock CSV/JSON gives coverage percentages and example gap dates. New dated packs are assembled on demand; this backfill does not certify every historical day as research-ready.

Downloads remain bounded by the project's PDF byte/page limits and public-source availability. A failed attachment stays unavailable. OCR text that cannot support a financial claim remains a limitation. The existing adverse-scan source cap remains enforced; this job does not silently declare an oversized or unreadable scan clean.

Commercial newsletters, subscription archives and every historical press story are not bundled. Reviewed licensed news/newsletter excerpts can still be imported through the existing dated workflow. Original publication verification and the case cutoff remain required.

Open **Policy Knowledge → Historical company evidence library**. The panel shows the company catalogue separately from policy-passage counts, including source counts and per-stock financial coverage. Download the coverage JSON there for the complete machine-readable audit.

Local artifacts:

- `reports/universe_evidence_coverage_before.json`: pack-readiness baseline before repair.
- `reports/universe_evidence_coverage_after.json` and `.csv`: final per-stock coverage, dated gap examples and limits.
- `data/company_research/issuer_archives/`: reusable per-stock source metadata and document/repair manifests.

Financial coverage percentages use each stock's actually available evaluation-session dates and eligible source timestamps; missing pre-listing OHLCV sessions are not counted as evidence failures. They measure one layer of source availability, not overall C1/C2 acceptance.

## Use the refreshed evidence in a demo

1. Refresh the dashboard and confirm API version 0.25.1.
2. Inspect the library panel and relevant stock's coverage/gap entries.
3. **Start a fresh C1 or C2 run.** Refreshed corpus and company-pack hashes invalidate old research approvals; do not mix the new evidence into a partly evaluated old portfolio.
4. Keep the existing chosen universe, capital and risk rules. This task does not change them.
5. Load a new research trace and inspect company sources, availability dates, source hashes, OCR cautions and scan readiness. Current-case company_evidence should retrieve eligible cached documents first; missing sources can still invoke the existing bounded web tool.
6. A real adverse finding, unresolved risk, missing required support, invalid data or absent technical pattern can still block entry. More documents do not justify forcing a trade or claiming improved profit.

Downloaded data remains local and is excluded from Git. A GitHub clone alone does not contain this historical library. Copy the data folder privately or repeat the source backfill on the demo machine.

## Repeat the backfill without generation calls

From the project directory:

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m scripts.backfill_universe_evidence --universe all --phase metadata --workers 3
.venv/bin/python -m scripts.backfill_universe_evidence --universe all --phase documents --ocr --workers 4
.venv/bin/python -m scripts.backfill_universe_evidence --universe all --phase repair --workers 3
.venv/bin/python -m scripts.audit_evidence_library --stage after
```

The document phase resumes completed issuer manifests and verified attachments. OCR is optional and requires macOS/Swift/Vision; elsewhere omit `--ocr` and inspect remaining extraction gaps. Source downloads use public NSE access, not OpenRouter or ChatGPT plan allowance. The policy index can be rebuilt separately with the existing `scripts.build_rag_index` command; no answer-generation call is required.
